### How It Works (Informative Part)

This animation visualizes two key types of energy hitting the tree:

1.  **Visible Light (Yellow Rays):** The solid yellow lines represent visible light. This is the primary energy source for **Photosynthesis**. The tree uses pigments (like chlorophyll in the leaves) to absorb this light, converting water and CO₂ into sugar (food) and oxygen.

2.  **UV Radiation (Dashed Purple Rays):** The dashed purple lines represent **Ultraviolet (UV) Radiation**. It is an invisible, high-energy wave.
    * *Informative:* While we show it here in purple, UV light is invisible to the human eye.
    * *The Danger:* Just like UV rays can give you a sunburn, they can also damage the tree's DNA and photosystems.
    * *The Protection:* Trees can’t put on sunscreen, but they produce their own "solar pigments" (like flavonoids). These compounds act as a protective barrier within the leaves, absorbing the UV radiation before it can harm the vital parts of the plant.

### The Animation Technology

This whole animation is actually **one single SVG image** embedded within a small JavaScript component. We use **CSS Keyframes** to animate different parts:
* `swayLeaves` rotates the entire leaves group back and forth.
* `uvFlow` animates the `stroke-dashoffset` of the purple lines, making them look like they are streaming towards the leaves.
